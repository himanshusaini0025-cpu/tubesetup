How to Use the YouTube Title Generator

Your title is the first thing viewers read and one of the strongest signals about what your video covers. A clear, specific title earns more clicks from the right people and helps YouTube understand the video.

What This Tool Does

Enter your video topic, pick a style (or leave it on "All styles") and the generator writes title ideas around your own words. Styles include how-to, list, curiosity, question and comparison. Press "More Titles" any time for new wording and numbers that were not shown before.

How to Use It

  1. Type your video topic — the more specific, the better the titles.
  2. Choose a style (optional) or keep "All styles" to see a mix.
  3. Click "Generate Titles" and read through the list. Click any title to copy it.
  4. Not feeling them? Press "More Titles" for fresh ideas as many times as you like.
  5. Edit your favorite so it is accurate to your video, then paste it into YouTube Studio.
Example: Topic "Sourdough Bread" with the style "How-to" can give titles like How to Sourdough Bread in 5 Easy Steps or The Simplest Way to Sourdough Bread (Step by Step).

Tips for Better Titles

  • Put your main keyword near the beginning of the title.
  • Keep titles under about 60 characters so they are not cut off in search and suggested videos.
  • Promise something specific the video really delivers.
  • Test a few styles — what works for one topic may not work for another.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Misleading clickbait. It hurts watch time and viewer trust.
  • Keyword stuffing. Titles should read naturally.
  • Using a generated title unchanged. Adjust the wording so it matches your video and your voice.
