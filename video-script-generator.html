How to Write a YouTube Script That Keeps People Watching

A good script is the difference between a video people leave after ten seconds and one they watch to the end. The YouTube Video Script Generator gives you a structured first draft in seconds, so you can spend your time on the part only you can do: adding your own voice, examples and expertise.

What your script includes

  • Hook: the opening line that gives viewers a reason to stay in the first few seconds.
  • Introduction: a short welcome that says what the video covers.
  • Main content: key points for a long video, quick points for Shorts, or numbered steps for a tutorial.
  • Transitions: linking lines that move the viewer from one part to the next.
  • Conclusion and call to action: a wrap-up, then a clear ask to like, subscribe or comment.

How to use the generator

  1. Type your video topic, for example “how to start a podcast”.
  2. Pick a video type, a script length, a language and a tone.
  3. Press Generate Script. Not happy with it? Press Regenerate for a fresh version, or More scripts to add new versions below.
  4. Copy the script or download it as a text file and start filming.

Make the draft your own

Treat the output as a skeleton. Replace the general lines with a real example from your own experience wherever you see the prompt to add one, read the script out loud, and cut anything that doesn't sound like you. For Shorts, aim for around 150 spoken words or fewer, and put your strongest point in the first sentence.

Tip: specific topics give better scripts. “Budget laptops for students” works better than just “laptops”.
